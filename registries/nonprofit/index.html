Public mirror of the IRS Form 990-series e-file returns as the original XML, one file per return keyed by the IRS object id, on a public S3 bucket.

Guidance

Best for: Employee and volunteer counts (Part I lines 5-6) · Any line of the return, byte for byte, for quote-checked facts · Foundation grant lists (990-PF, Schedule I) for funder research

Routing: discovery: not_applicable citation: preferred quantitative_claims: preferred

staff count for a year return-xml
object_id from ProPublica organization.latest_object_id (or its filings); read TaxYr and TotalEmployeeCnt / TotalVolunteersCnt.
--param object_id=202611629349301406
⚠ TotalEmployeeCnt is the number of W-2s issued in the calendar year, not staff or FTEs. For an orchestra it includes every per-service musician, so it can be far larger than the office staff.
⚠ TaxYr is the year the fiscal year began: TaxYr 2024 with TaxPeriodEndDt 2025-06-30 is FY2024-25. Label figures by the period dates.
⚠ The file starts with a UTF-8 BOM; strip it before XML parsing, but quote from the stored bytes.
⚠ Paper filings and very recent returns are absent; a 404 means not mirrored, not 'no return'.

Searchable republication of IRS exempt-organization data: the EO Business Master File and the IRS SOI annual extract of Form 990 financial fields, with links to each filing. Keyless JSON API.

Guidance

Best for: Finding an organization's EIN and every filing year · Headline revenue, expenses, assets and contributions by tax year · latest_object_id, the key to the full IRS XML return

Routing: discovery: preferred citation: follow_primary quantitative_claims: acceptable → resolve via source_of_record (Cite the filed Form 990 (EIN, tax period, object id); ProPublica is where it was found.)

EIN from a name org-search
q=<name>; check city/state and NTEE (A69 = symphony orchestras) before trusting a hit.
--param q="Berkeley Symphony"
financial history organization
ein=<digits>; filings_with_data[] newest first; tax_prd_yr is the year the fiscal year STARTED (tax_prd 202307 = FY Jul 2023–Jun 2024).
--param ein=237219508
⚠ No employee or volunteer counts: those are in the full return (Part I lines 5-6); take latest_object_id to the IRS e-file XML (990 data lake).

⚠ filings_with_data lags the filings by a year or more; filings_without_data lists newer returns that only have a PDF.
⚠ formtype: 0 = 990, 1 = 990-EZ, 2 = 990-PF.
